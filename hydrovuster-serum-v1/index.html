About this item

  • Hydration, Measured: One use took skin moisture to 7.3× its starting level. After fifteen rounds of tape stripping, it still read 4.9× (22 women, forearm; temporary, results vary. Korea Institute of Dermatological Sciences, 2026).
  • Why It’s Blue: Blame guaiazulene, at 1,000 ppm. No dyes added, so the shade can vary a little from batch to batch.
  • What’s in the Bottle: PDRN 10,000 ppm (Derbiome® PDRN 10K raw material, about 100 ppm sodium DNA), snail mucin 3,000 ppm, niacinamide, and ectoin.
  • Patch-Tested: Scored 0.00 for irritation in a 24-hour patch test (32 adults), with zero skin reactions in the use test (Korea Institute of Dermatological Sciences, 2026).
  • Three Steps, Done: Cleanse and tone, press in a few drops morning and night, then lock it in with cream. Keep it away from the eye area.
