About this item

  • Seals In Moisture: In a clinical test of 21 adults with dry skin, one application cut water loss through the skin by an average of 28.8% and raised skin hydration by 83% (Korea Institute of Dermatological Sciences, 2026). Results are temporary and vary
  • What's Inside: Derbiome PDRN 10K at 5,000 ppm (about 50 ppm as sodium DNA), panthenol 3% and niacinamide 2%, plus ceramide NP and squalane
  • Spreads Thin, Feels Light: A soft, light blue cream for the last step. In the same test, every participant liked the texture and said their skin felt like it was holding on to moisture
  • Patch-Tested: 0.00 mean irritation score (non-irritant) in a 24-hour patch test on 32 adults, graded by a dermatologist, plus zero skin reactions in a 21-person single-application study (Korea Institute of Dermatological Sciences, 2026)
  • Last Step, Morning and Night: After toner and serum, smooth a small amount over the face, then cup your palms to help it set. Avoid the eye area and broken skin

Questions & Answers

Is it heavy?

No. It's a soft cream that spreads thin, so a little covers your whole face.

Where does it go in my routine?

Last, after toner and serum. In the morning, follow with sunscreen.

Can I use it every day?

Yes, morning and night. If your skin is sensitive, patch test first, and stop use if irritation occurs.

Why does the blue look a little different from tube to tube?

The light blue shade can vary slightly from batch to batch and may shift with heat and light. It does not affect quality.

Does it contain fragrance?

No fragrance is listed in the ingredients. See the full ingredient list in the images.
