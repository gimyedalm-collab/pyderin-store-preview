질문 칸 (FAQ)

Why does skin temperature matter?

In direct sun, skin can reach about 104°F. Dermatologists at Seoul National University found that heat sets off enzymes that break down collagen.

Is the PDRN vegan?

No, and that's on purpose. Ours is salmon-derived Derbiome® PDRN 10K at 99% purity.

When should I use it?

Whenever skin feels hot: after the beach, a workout or a long night in the kitchen.

How fast does it work?

We measured skin temperature right after one 20-minute use.

What do I do after?

Pat in the leftover essence, then follow with your moisturizer.
